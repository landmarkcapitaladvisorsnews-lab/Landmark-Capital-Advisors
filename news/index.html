Our Core Investment & Asset Management Services

1. Alternative Investment Funds (AIF)

Alternative Investment Funds provide a structured mechanism for participating in specialised investment opportunities. Landmark Capital Advisors manages Category II AIFs designed around defined investment strategies and real estate opportunities.

Its investment platform includes Landmark Opportunity Fund and Landmark Multiplier Fund, reflecting its focus on professionally managed investment structures.

2. Real Estate Asset Management

Effective asset management plays an important role in determining the long-term performance of real estate investments. It requires continuous evaluation of asset-level requirements, operational performance and changing market conditions.

Landmark Capital Advisors focuses on value creation throughout the asset lifecycle, combining strategic oversight with operational involvement. This approach supports informed decisions around asset development, performance and investment objectives.

3. Managed Accounts

Different investors have different financial objectives, investment horizons and risk considerations. Managed accounts offer a customized investment approach that considers individual portfolio requirements.

Landmark Capital Advisors offers managed account capabilities for high-net-worth investors seeking tailored real estate investment strategies and professional portfolio oversight.

4. Special Situations Investment

Certain real estate opportunities emerge from complex financial, operational or market-specific circumstances. These situations require detailed evaluation, structured capital and specialised execution capabilities.

Landmark Capital Advisors evaluates special situation opportunities where operational expertise and an appropriate investment structure can play an important role in unlocking underlying asset potential.
